## How you'll work

<div class="flow">

- **You** Say what you want, in everyday words
- **Your agent** Writes the app, its database tools and its connections
- **Fresh Breath** Hosts it, signs people in and proxies its calls
- **Your live app** Opens in a browser at its own address

</div>
